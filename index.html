Understanding LIC

Life Insurance Corporation of India (LIC) is a life insurance provider. Life insurance can help families manage the financial impact of the insured person's death, while some plans also support savings, long-term goals or retirement planning according to their terms.

🛡️

Financial protection for family

A life insurance policy may provide a death benefit to the nominee if the insured person dies during the policy period, subject to policy conditions.

காப்பீடு பெற்றவர் இறந்தால், பாலிசி நிபந்தனைகளுக்கு உட்பட்டு நியமனதாரருக்கு நிதி உதவி கிடைக்கலாம்.

🎯

Planning for future goals

Depending on the plan, customers may choose options designed for savings, a child's future, regular income or retirement needs.

சேமிப்பு, குழந்தைகளின் எதிர்காலம் மற்றும் ஓய்வூதியத் தேவைகளுக்கான திட்டங்களைப் பற்றி அறியலாம்.

Life insurance is not a one-size-fits-all product. Benefits, exclusions, premium, eligibility and payout conditions differ by plan. Always review the official policy brochure and benefit illustration before making a decision.

Explore Policy Categories

Start with the type of cover or financial goal you want to explore. Ganesan can help you understand relevant options and their official terms.

🛡️

Term Insurance

Life cover for a selected policy term, subject to policy conditions.

💰

Endowment & Money Back

Plans that combine life cover with savings or scheduled survival benefits, as specified in the policy.

🎓

Child Plans

Options designed to help parents plan for a child's future milestones.

🌅

Pension & Annuity

Plans that can support retirement income planning, according to selected options.

🌳

Whole Life

Life insurance options that may provide cover over a longer or whole-life period, as defined by the plan.

📈

Market-Linked Plans

Plans where returns are linked to market performance and investment risk is borne by the policyholder.

➕

Riders & Additional Benefits

Optional add-ons may be available with eligible base policies, subject to terms.
